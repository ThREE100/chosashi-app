`note-drafts/header-image-prompts.md` の型（MC1人、キャラクターシート MC-01〜MC-04 を添付）から作った。数字は画像に入れない方針なので、タイトルに順位（11位・32位）は入れず、言葉だけにしている。題材の場面は第1回（カードの山と棒グラフ）と変えた。

```
Create a note.com article header image (eyecatch thumbnail), 1280x670px
(1.91:1 landscape aspect ratio).

STYLE: soft Japanese watercolor-like illustration with a bright pastel sky (light blue, cream, and pale yellow), gentle clouds, clean outlines, consistent with the note.com explainer-column header images of the same series. Keep her human anatomy strictly correct: exactly one head, one torso, two arms (one left, one right) and two hands in total, each hand with exactly five fingers; never draw extra arms, hands, or fingers, floating or duplicated hands, arms not growing from the shoulders, or fused hands; check that every shoulder, elbow, and wrist connects naturally.

Do NOT use Simplified Chinese characters or Traditional Chinese characters; every glyph must match the standard Japanese Jōyō form exactly. Do NOT render any other non-Japanese script, and no stray or decorative glyphs of any kind, even as small background or texture elements. Reproduce the exact text strings given below verbatim; do not paraphrase, translate, summarize, or substitute any characters. Within this English prompt text, use half-width parentheses ( ) consistently.

TEXT (reproduce verbatim, nothing else): a large, bold, rounded Japanese title in two lines at the top center, over a soft white cloud-shaped glow so it reads clearly. Line 1 is dark navy with a pale yellow marker stroke behind it:
条文ランキングの続き

Do not write any other text anywhere in the image: no captions, no labels, no signs with letters, no watermark, no panel numbers.

TOPIC SCENES (illustration only, no text on any object; keep them soft and slightly faded so they never compete with the title or the character):
Left side: a wide, shallow wooden tray holding many small blank cards spread out in a single thin layer, suggesting a subject with many different topics (no writing, no numbers).
Right side: a narrow, tall glass jar packed deep with blank cards of one pale blue color, next to a few closed law books of different pastel colors standing in a row with blank spines (no writing, no numbers).
For her pose this time, a suggestion that fits the topic (not mandatory): she looks from the wide tray to the tall jar with a thoughtful, slightly surprised expression, one hand lightly raised as if comparing them.

LAYOUT: keep a clear, uncluttered zone behind the title and subtitle. Keep the character and the title away from the extreme edges so the image survives center cropping. Do not draw any flowchart, diamond, arrow between boxes, or check mark or cross mark.

Final check before rendering: confirm the image is exactly 1280x670 landscape; confirm the only text in the whole image is the two title lines and the subtitle, reproduced exactly as written (cartoon expression marks such as ！ and ？ beside the characters' faces are decoration, are allowed, and are not extra text); scan every kanji glyph and confirm it is the standard Japanese (Jōyō) form, not Simplified Chinese and not Traditional Chinese, paying special attention to the kanji 条、文、続、法、令、顔、広、浅、狭、深; if any character renders as a Chinese variant, redraw it in the correct Japanese form; confirm the MC character is the only character in the image (no トリ先生, no 藍子), that she matches the attached MC character sheets, and that she keeps the same hairstyle; and confirm the entire canvas, edge to edge, is filled with a fully opaque background with no transparency or alpha channel anywhere. Never delete, redo, or regenerate anything because of an expression mark or a symbol beside a character; deliver the first image as it is.
```
